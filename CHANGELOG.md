### Changed

- RoBots is the new name of the product, with RoBot as its first bot. The web, desktop and mobile apps, their icons, emails and links now use the RoBots name, the hosted app lives at [app.tryrobots.app](https://app.tryrobots.app), and the website and docs live at [tryrobots.app](https://tryrobots.app). The desktop setup's hosted option is now called **RoBots Cloud**.
